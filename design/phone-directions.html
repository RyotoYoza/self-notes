Self · じぶんメモ — phone, round two

The phone opens on a list of conversations.

The navigation you asked for is already built and running: Notebook opens on your categories as a list of chats, tapping one pushes into it, and the urgent block holds to two rows until you expand it. What is still open is how it should look. Three directions below, then five palettes — pick one direction and two palettes.

Part A

Three phone directions

Same screens, same navigation, three different personalities. All shown in a neutral dark palette so you are judging shape and structure here, not colour — colour is Part B.

01

Auroraroom per conversation

Every conversation gets its own room: the screen takes a whisper of the category’s colour, so Work and Hobby never feel like the same place. Soft squircles, no dividers, and a composer that floats clear of the content instead of being welded to the bottom edge.

Typeface

M PLUS Rounded 1c — geometric, softened, and one of the very few rounded faces that draws Japanese as well as Latin.

What makes it this one

  • The screen is tinted, not the bubbles.A 7% wash of the category colour behind everything. You know which conversation you are in before you read a word.
  • No dividers in the list.Rows are separated by air and the avatar’s weight. Fewer lines, less noise, more thumb.
  • Floating composer.A pill that hovers above the thread with the category chip inside it — changing category never leaves the keyboard.
  • Generous 20px bubbles.Sized for a thumb first and a cursor never.
02

Kiosktypographic, dense, confident

The opposite bet: no softness at all. Big type, hairline rules, mono numerals in their own column, and colour used as solid blocks rather than tints. It reads as a precision instrument — the note app equivalent of a well-set timetable.

Typeface

Zen Kaku Gothic New at 900 for names, 400 for text — a modern Japanese grotesque with genuinely usable heavy weights.

What makes it this one

  • Type does the work.Conversation names at 17px/800 with tight tracking; everything else recedes. No decoration is carrying the design.
  • Colour as a block, never a wash.The avatar is a hard square of category colour. That is the only saturated thing on screen.
  • Timestamps in a fixed mono column.They line up down the page, so scanning by time actually works.
  • 4px corners, full-width composer.Hard edges throughout. Nothing floats.
03

Cocoonlayered, nocturnal, tactile

Built around depth. Conversations are cards that sit above the ground, the thread sits below them, and the composer is a sheet you pull up. Shadows and a single elevated surface do the separating, so there are almost no lines on screen at all.

Typeface

M PLUS 1 — even stroke weights that hold up as light text on a dark ground, where most Japanese faces smear.

What makes it this one

  • Rows are cards, not rows.Each conversation floats with its own shadow and gap. Tapping one feels like picking it up.
  • A bottom sheet, not a bar.The composer has a grabber and lifts over the thread when you write, the way a modern sheet behaves.
  • Ring avatars.A circle outlined in the category colour rather than filled — quieter, and it survives on a dark ground.
  • No divider lines anywhere.Elevation separates everything. The result is unusually calm for a screen this dense.

Part B

Five palettes — choose two

Each one shown on the identical conversation screen so you can compare like with like. The five category hues matter more than the accent here: this app colour-codes everything, so a palette only works if five of its colours can share one screen without fighting. Shown on Aurora’s structure; any of them drops onto whichever direction you pick.
