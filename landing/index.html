The idea

Notes stop working the moment they feel like filing.

Ask someone to decide *where* a thought goes and they stop writing it down. A chat never asks that. You just send it.

01

Send it

Tap a colour, hit send. Two taps, done. It feels less like writing and more like telling someone.

02

It files itself

The moment it’s sent it’s already in the right conversation. There is no “sort this out later” step.

03

It stays yours

It never leaves your device. No account, no server. Export the whole thing to one file whenever you like.

How it works

There are only really two screens.

One where you send things to yourself. One where the things you sent are waiting. That’s it.

Step 01 — Sending

Pick a colour, then send.

Choosing a category is required. It sounds like friction — it’s the reason the “I’ll organise this later” pile never appears.

The rest of it

The things you’d check before installing.

Install it like an app

Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app — and works with no signal.

English and 日本語

The whole app, both languages, switchable any time. Enter never fires mid-conversion on a Japanese IME.

Five palettes

Light ones, dark ones, and one that follows your device when it goes dark.

Deleting is reversible by design

A delete marks the note, it never shreds the record — so a future sync can never resurrect it by accident.

Export the whole thing

Every note and category as one file. Importing merges — it never overwrites what’s already there.

No account, no server

No sign-up, no login, no inbox to leak. Your notes stay on your device.

Next time you think of something, just send it to yourself.

Nothing to sign up for. It works the second you open it.

Open the app

On iPhone: open it in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. Safari can clear the data of a site you haven’t opened in 7 days; an installed app isn’t treated that way.
